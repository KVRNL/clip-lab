## What it does

Clip Lab turns hours-long recordings into shareable clips fast. Open an OBS capture (or any MP4, MKV, MOV and more), scrub to the moment, mark a Start and End, name it, and add it to the queue — then batch-export the whole list at once. Even a six-hour file opens instantly because Clip Lab reads it in place.

Leave it on instant, lossless cuts, or flip on frame-perfect mode for exact edges — it re-encodes on your GPU (NVIDIA NVENC) and falls back to your CPU automatically. Everything runs locally, and you can even run several copies at once, one per video. Free to use; claim your license key and download it straight from this page.

## Features

- **Instant, lossless cuts — or frame-perfect re-encodes**

## What's new

**v1.0.17** — 2026-10-11
  - New close button in the top-right corner: close the video you're done with and go back to the start screen without quitting the app.
  - The Choose Video button is now a folder icon, next to the new close button, so the four buttons in the top-right corner match.
  - If there are clips in your list when you close a video, Clip Lab asks first, since they were marked on that video. An export that's already running keeps going and finishes normally.

**v1.0.16** — 2026-10-11
  - The timeline rows are now the layer order: one row per item, with the front-most at the top. Drag a bar up or down to change what's in front. The numbers update as you drag, and the video re-layers with it.
  - Each drag goes one way: sideways changes the timing, up or down changes the layer, so restacking never moves the timing by accident.
  - Fixed: pressing a bar again right after clicking it now drags it, instead of jumping into its text box. A double-click still does that.

**v1.0.15** — 2026-10-11
  - New: full layer control. A Layer order list in the side panel shows everything on the video, front-most at the top. Drag rows to restack them, or use their arrows.
  - Right-click a bar for Bring forward and Send backward (one step) as well as Bring to front and Send to back. Ctrl+] and Ctrl+[ move one step, add Shift to go all the way.
  - Each bar on the timeline shows its place in the stack, and a new Layers button on the timeline opens the layer order any time.
  - Nearly 600 stickers, sorted into groups: Popular, Faces, Hands and people, Hearts, Symbols, Fun and gaming, Animals and nature, Food and drink, and Travel.
  - The timeline's scrollbar is slimmer.

**v1.0.14** — 2026-10-11
  - The video and everything under it now use the whole window, so the picture is much bigger.
  - Your clips list moved to a new button next to Settings. It shows how many clips are waiting, and opens the list as a popup like Settings.
  - Exports can run in the background: hide the progress window and keep working. The button shows the progress, then a check mark when it's done (or ! if something failed), and a message on the video lets you see the result.
  - The text, sticker and shape editor now slides in on the right while you're editing and slides away when you're done.

**v1.0.13** — 2026-10-11
  - A cleaner, more compact timeline. Bars show their text or icon right on them, and things that don't overlap in time share a row, so the video gets more room.
  - Bars now line up on their own: they snap to whole seconds, the playhead, and the start or end of other bars, with a yellow line showing what they lined up with. Shift still gives tenths of a second.
  - While you drag, a bubble shows exactly when it starts and ends. Hover a bar to duplicate or delete it, or right-click for more, including Bring to front and Send to back. Ctrl+D duplicates.
  - A new look for the export window: a clear status at the top, a progress bar for the whole export, and a card for each clip with a Show button once it's saved.
